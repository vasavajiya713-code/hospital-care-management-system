About Us

Hospital Introduction

City Care Hospital was established with a vision to provide affordable, high quality healthcare to every family in the region. Over the years, we have grown into a multi specialty hospital equipped with modern technology and a team of skilled doctors.

Our Mission

To deliver compassionate, timely, and effective medical care to every patient who walks through our doors.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted healthcare provider in the region, known for excellence and integrity.

Our Services

  • 24/7 Emergency Care
  • Advanced Diagnostic Labs
  • Specialist Consultations
  • In Patient and Out Patient Services
  • Ambulance Services

Hospital History

Founded in 1998, City Care Hospital began as a small clinic and has since expanded into a full fledged hospital serving thousands of patients every year.
